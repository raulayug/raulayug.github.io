About Me

I grew up with music and computers. I considered myself quite tech-savvy and was enamored with a game called Minecraft, especially its redstone feature. At the same time, I started drum lessons, building a foundation in music.

In high school, I got into a lot of creative software and taught myself how to edit videos and create posters. I created promotional material for school projects and organizations. There, I developed a passion for event production and became a foundational part of my school fair's flagship benefit concerts.

In college, I took up Computer Science with a specialization in Interactive Multimedia. I joined the Ateneo Musicians' Pool as a member and eventually became the Vice President. At the same time, I started RausHaus, a mix of a music studio, production house, and events organizer.

Recently, I finished my master's, with my thesis leveraging my knowledge of both music production and software development. I designed a real-time voice gate plugin prototype, powered by a custom deep learning model to solve a problem I've encountered as an audio engineer.

I'm currently exploring my next venture that empowers me to be the technical behind the creative.
